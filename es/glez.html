Case Studies

Selected Work

Five pieces of the founder's work: two from St. James International, two open-source projects and one academic project.

01
Portfolio Optimization · St. James International
Portfolio Management Software
⏱ 4h → 30min per client · Used by my team
Details
+

Challenge

Preparing an optimal allocation took more than four hours per client, which limited how many clients each adviser could serve.

Approach

Applied Bloomberg Terminal data with Fama-French 5-factor model and Efficient Portfolio Theory. Automated selection using graph theory and log regression — first in R, then refactored to Python for performance.

PythonRBloombergFama-FrenchGraph Theory

Impact

Cut preparation from more than four hours per client to under 30 minutes. Used by the founder and his team at St. James International, so they could serve more clients with the same people.

02
Credit Risk · Open-source project
Credit Risk PD Model
🎯 Scorecard, challenger and bank-style validation report
Details
+

Challenge

Banks need probability-of-default models that rank borrowers well, give the right level of risk, stay stable over time and can be explained to a model-validation team.

Approach

Weight-of-evidence binning and information-value selection feed a logistic-regression scorecard scaled to points. A gradient-boosting challenger, explained with SHAP, is compared against it. Everything is fitted on the development sample only.

scikit-learnPythonFinancial RatiosWoE Scorecard

Impact

An automatic validation report covers discrimination (Gini, KS), calibration (Hosmer-Lemeshow), stability (PSI) and default rates by rating grade, with plain-language findings and limitations.

03
Quantitative Trading · Open-source project
Market Prediction & Backtesting System
📈 Walk-forward backtest with transaction costs
Details
+

Challenge

Testing whether simple price features carry a usable short-term signal, without look-ahead bias.

Approach

Random-forest forecasts of five-day returns from momentum and volatility features, turned into long/flat signals and tested walk-forward with a five-day embargo and transaction costs.

Random ForestPythonBacktestingSignal GenerationTime Series

Impact

A reproducible research pipeline that reports results after costs, including when the signal does not beat buy-and-hold.

04
Real Estate · Academic project
Apartment-Mix Optimisation
🎓 Log-linear regression on comparable sales
Details
+

Challenge

Case study: find the mix of luxury and standard apartments that maximises a development's profit, without reliable local demand data.

Approach

Scraped and analyzed comparable sales data using MySQL. Applied EDA and Log-Linear Regression in R to quantify price drivers and predict demand elasticity by unit type.

R StudioMySQLLog-Linear RegressionEDA

Impact

The model ranked candidate unit mixes by expected profit and showed the main price drivers. An academic exercise, not a client engagement.

05
Wealth Management · St. James International
Client Portfolio Advisory
📊 Most profitable portfolios in the firm, 2016–18 · first of 20 advisers
Details
+

Challenge

Individual clients required personalized investment strategies balancing risk tolerance, liquidity needs, and return expectations — while remaining competitive against market benchmarks across volatile periods.

Approach

Deployed proprietary portfolio software alongside Excel and Power BI. Applied Fama-French 3 and 5-factor models combined with behavioral finance principles to tailor each portfolio to the client's unique risk-return profile.

Fama-FrenchPower BIExcelBehavioral Finance

Impact

Ran the firm's most profitable portfolios in 2016–18, first of 20 advisers, for about 60 clients with USD 45M in assets.
